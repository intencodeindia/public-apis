Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use an API listed here?
Click "Explore API" to visit the documentation and get your endpoint, then use it in your frontend, backend, or with our playground above.
Are these APIs free to use?
Yes, all APIs listed here are free to use. Some may require registration or have usage limits—check their docs.
Can I contribute more APIs?
Absolutely! Click "Contribute an API" or see the Contributors section above.
Is there a limit to how many APIs I can use?
No, you can use as many as you like! Each API may have its own request limits—see their docs for more info.
